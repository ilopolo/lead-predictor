# LeadScore scorecard

LeadScore uses explicit signals so every recommendation can be discussed and adjusted.

| Signal | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Company size: 1–10 | 10 |
| Company size: 11–50 | 24 |
| Company size: 51–250 | 35 |
| Company size: 251+ | 45 |
| Budget: exploring | 15 |
| Budget: likely | 30 |
| Budget: confirmed | 45 |
| Intent keyword | 5 each, up to 15 |

## Priority bands

- **75–100:** contact within one business day.
- **50–74:** send a targeted follow-up.
- **Below 50:** nurture with a relevant resource.
